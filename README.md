# Using Physical Models to Understand Water Vapor Feedback and Its Impacts

A 2024 summer theoretical and literature-based project on physical models of water-vapor feedback.

## Full paper

[Using Physical Models to Understand Water Vapor Feedback and Its Impacts (PDF)](paper/manuscript.pdf) · [Editable manuscript](paper/manuscript.docx)

Author: **Yi Kang**. The project was prepared in the context of *The Atmosphere and Climate*, taught by R. Alan Plumb. The instructor is not presented as a coauthor.

## Scope and reproducibility

This repository contains the complete available final paper. No dedicated computational implementation or study dataset was found with the final project. Reproduction consists of following the paper's physical arguments, equations and cited literature; this repository does not claim an executable numerical replication.

Unrelated exercises, textbooks, lecture materials, administrative forms and personal information have been excluded. The original paper language and citations are preserved.
